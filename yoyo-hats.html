Every brand begins with a single line. For YOYO, it was three loops of pen on graph paper.

YOYO Hats is a boutique label crafting modern silhouettes for a new generation of hat lovers. The brief asked for an identity that felt confident, feminine, and a little playful — a brand that takes itself seriously, but not too seriously.

The logo grew from a single loose sketch: a side-profile face wearing two intersecting brims. From there, the lines were tightened, the proportions refined, and the colour story (deep teal, magenta, soft skin) locked in. The finished mark works as comfortably on a circular shop sign as it does on a label, business card, or Instagram avatar.
