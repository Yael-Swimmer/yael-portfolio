The brief

5K Runner is the coaching app that's helped more than 5 million beginners finish their first 5K. The product was already loved — what it needed was a marketing website that felt as motivating as the workout itself.

"Every visitor should feel like they could lace up tomorrow."

The approach

The site needed to convert curious visitors into runners in under thirty seconds. The design prioritises a single, confident promise above the fold — "The simplest way to run 5K" — paired with a single call to action.

Illustrated runners (designed in-house) carry the visual identity across every section, gently turning what could be intimidating into something playful and human. The colour system leans on open sky blues and warm coral accents — energetic, optimistic, and unmistakably outdoors.

The outcome

Apple selected the app as App of the Day. The website became the first touch-point for hundreds of thousands of new users, turning a couch-to-5K promise into a clear, beautiful, and confidence-building journey.
